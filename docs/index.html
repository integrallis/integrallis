Position

What we commit to, and where we say no.

The frontier is not the only axis of progress. The more consequential shift is intelligence getting small enough, cheap enough, and local enough to sit inside ordinary systems — next to the data, on the device, under the owner's control. Three convictions follow from that, and everything here is an attempt to test them.

  1. 01Ownership

    Everything we build runs on infrastructure you already control: inside your process, on your object storage, on your device. Retrieval, inference, and memory stay inside the boundary you already defend.

  2. 02Evidence

    Nothing ships on a model's confidence. Models are qualified against measured capability, code is scored deterministically, builds pass on real execution, and our papers publish their own noise floors.

  3. 03The boundary

    Local execution is not universal. Model size, memory, latency, and hardware decide where it stops working — and we would rather publish that line than pretend it isn't there.
